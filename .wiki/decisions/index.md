# Decision

* [A source must answer a GitHub runner, and a refusal is not routed around](0001-sources-must-answer-a-github-runner.md) - The daily build runs on GitHub-hosted runners, so a source that refuses them (Eventbrite, 405) is removed rather than reached another way, and a 403 is recorded as a failure, never worked around with curl.
* [Haiku readers on 30,000-character pieces, a Sonnet editor](0003-haiku-readers-and-a-sonnet-editor.md) - The daily run reads with Haiku on pieces of at most 30,000 characters and edits with Sonnet, by the user's decision of 2026-10-21 and against the evaluation's advice: about $1.15 a run against about $3, with more fact slips.
* [No Viral Agenda](0004-no-viral-agenda.md) - Viral Agenda would add about 22 events over two to three weeks but refuses the collector's HTTP client with a 403 whatever the User-Agent, so it is left out and not reached another way.
* [Ship three intermediate certificates, keep verification on](0002-ship-three-intermediate-certificates.md) - Three servers send an incomplete TLS chain that OpenSSL on Linux cannot complete, so certs/intermediates.pem adds their intermediates next to the system roots; no root of ours, never verification off.
