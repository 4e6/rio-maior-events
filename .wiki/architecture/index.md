# Module

* [Rio Maior events](rio-maior-events.md) - A twice-weekly build that gathers leisure events within about 45 minutes of Rio Maior and renders an agenda list beside a month calendar. Why a script collects and the model reads, which sources, how the page works, where it bends the payload rules.
* [Rio Maior events, the sources](rio-maior-events-sources.md) - The maintainer's view of each source the rio-maior-events collector reads: when and how it was checked, what to look at by hand, what breaks it, why it was chosen over its alternatives, and the sites that could not be read, by municipality.
